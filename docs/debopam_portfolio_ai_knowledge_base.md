# 5. Major Projects

## 5.1 BioRemed AI

### Full project concept

**BioRemed AI: A Context-Aware Environmental AI for Soil Contamination Diagnosis and Bioremediation Planning via Multimodal Machine Learning**

### What it is

BioRemed AI is an environmental intelligence system designed to combine soil measurements, environmental context, machine learning, and local AI reasoning to support:

- Soil contamination severity assessment
- Bioremediation strategy recommendation
- Environmental risk interpretation
- Context-aware remediation planning
- Monitoring recommendations

### Core idea

Instead of looking at a soil parameter in isolation, the system tries to understand a **multimodal environmental context**.

### Inputs

Potential inputs include:

- Soil pH
- Electrical conductivity
- Nitrogen
- Phosphorus
- Potassium
- Soil moisture
- Soil temperature
- Salinity
- Weather indicators
- Air-quality indicators
- Optional soil imagery
- Geographic context

### ML architecture

The concept uses a **dual-model approach**:

1. **Contamination severity classifier**
   - Estimates severity such as:
     - Mild
     - Moderate
     - Severe
     - Critical

2. **Mitigation / bioremediation classifier**
   - Predicts an appropriate remediation approach.

### Context enrichment

The project can incorporate environmental conditions such as:

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Wind
- Rainfall
- PM2.5
- PM10
- O₃
- NO₂
- SO₂
- CO

### Intelligent reasoning layer

A local LLM can be used to convert structured model outputs into a readable remediation plan.

The output can include:

- Risk classification
- Immediate interventions
- Phased treatment strategy
- Monitoring protocol
- Hazard warnings
- Explanation of the reasoning

### Edge AI direction

A major design goal is running meaningful inference locally where possible, reducing dependence on external APIs.

### Hardware direction

Potential hardware used or explored:

- Raspberry Pi
- Multi-parameter soil sensor
- Salinity sensing
- LiDAR
- Environmental / gas sensing
- Light sensing

### Future research directions

- IoT sensor arrays
- Heavy-metal speciation
- Satellite imagery
- Remote sensing
- Larger geospatial datasets
- Mobile deployment
- Advanced multimodal models
- Better uncertainty estimation
